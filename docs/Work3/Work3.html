CSS Landscape

For this assignment, we had to build an animated landscape using only CSS and HTML. No images, no JPEGs, no GIFs. Everything you see is made from code. Instead of going post apocalyptic, I made something colorful and dreamy. I used a purple sky with a bright orange gradient. The sun is my favorite part. Instead of a simple circle, I built it out of five rotating square layers in yellow, orange, and gold. It looks almost like a kaleidoscope or a celestial flower. I added two airplanes. The first one carries a banner that says ‘Hello!!' and the second one says 'Welcome To Rey's Landscape'. I gave them different colors, different animation speeds, and little ropes connecting the banners to the planes. The mountains have purple and green gradients, and I added three different sizes of trees scattered across the landscape. There is also a tall tower and a house with little blinking window lights that I coded using box shadows and an animation tag. Looking at my actual code, I am proud of how much I pushed this beyond the basic requirements. The sun alone took five nested divs with individual rotation degrees. This was the most technical project I have done in any art class, but seeing my name flying across my own coded landscape made it worth it.
