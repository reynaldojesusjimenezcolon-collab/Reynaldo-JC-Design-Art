About

Greetings, My name is Reynaldo Jimenez Colon and I am 23 years old, an accounting major, and I love art. Those two things might not always go together in people's minds, but for me, they do. Accounting teaches me precision, organization, and attention to detail. Art teaches me to see problems differently, take risks, and communicate effectively without words.

This site is a collection of work I have made over the last two years in my art courses. You will see projects ranging from silk screen T shirt designs to CSS coded landscapes, from print emboss experiments to two color film noir invitations. Every piece here represents a problem I had to solve, a tool I had to learn, or a constraint I had to work around.

Outside of school, I have worked as a cashier, I am fluent in Spanish. I play chess , I occasionally watch romance comedies ; and I am someone who believes that being an accountant who makes art is not a contradiction. It is just who I am.

The skills I have built in these art courses are not separate from my professional abilities. Learning Adobe Creative Suite (InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop) taught me how to manage complex projects with multiple layers and tight specifications. Learning HTML and CSS gave me a foundation in how websites are built from the ground up, no drag and drop, just structure and style written line by line. Designing within strict limitations like two color printing or no images forced me to be creative within boundaries, something every workplace requires. Building a CSS landscape from scratch with no external images trained me to be methodical, to debug my own work, and to see a project through to the finished product.

I do not see my art coursework as a hobby. I see it as proof that I can learn complex tools, follow strict guidelines, and still produce work that is thoughtful and original.

Thank you for visiting!
