Film Festival Invitation

For this project, I designed a two-sided invitation for a Film Noir Festival at the Film Forum theater. The assignment was focused on using a strict two-color Pantone system, so I worked with black and yellow throughout the design to create a dramatic noir-inspired look. I also had to incorporate both grayscale and duotone images, which pushed me to experiment with image editing and color treatment in Photoshop to make all of the film stills feel visually connected.

I chose six different films and selected one image from each to represent them. I arranged the layout to look like overlapping strips of film, which helped tie the movie theme into the overall composition while also creating movement across both sides of the invitation. I included the required film information, quotes, descriptive text, and directors names, while making sure the typography stayed readable within the busy layout.

One part of the assignment was designing an original Film Forum logo, which I created to match the retro cinema aesthetic of the piece. I also had to think carefully about hierarchy, balance, bleed, and printer's marks since this project was designed for print production. Through this assignment, I learned more about working within color limitations, creating duotone imagery, and designing for a professional print layout while still keeping the design visually engaging and cinematic.
