Emboss

For this project, we had to create an 8x8 single page print piece promoting the benefits of a specific animal. I chose a giant panda. The twist was that we had to make the animal's name a blind emboss, meaning raised texture with no ink, just to show a client what it would look like after printing.

I designed a little guide called 'Guide to Caring for a Panda.' It's honest. Pandas are high maintenance, messy, and expensive. They need a massive enclosure, 20 to 40 pounds of bamboo daily, and they produce up to 50 pounds of waste a day. But I also wanted to remind people that owning one, if you even could, is really a conservation effort. I pulled a panda image from online, built everything in InDesign, and used Photoshop to create that embossed look on the title.
